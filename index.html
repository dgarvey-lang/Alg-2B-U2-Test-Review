Teacher answer key ↗
How to play. The team in control picks a tile. Read the question aloud, then click Open Buzzers. Teams buzz in with their keyboard key (shown above) or by tapping their colored buzz pad on screen. The first team gets the timer to answer. Click Correct to award the points (that team picks next) or Incorrect — that team is locked out and buzzers reopen for everyone else. If nobody gets it, click Reveal Answer. Buzzing before the buzzers open locks that team out for 1 second. Use the small +/− buttons on a score to fix mistakes. The game saves automatically, so refreshing the page won't lose scores.
